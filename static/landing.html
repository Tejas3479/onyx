Rule 149(vii) compares against the Lowest-1 of the valid competitive pool, not the median — with a ±25% band and an explicit within/outside verdict on the primary price.
Procurement Threshold Compliance

Statutory Application Stages

Built for every procurement milestone

Two mandatory application points mandated by the problem statement's Clause 2.1.3.

Stage 1

Administrative Approval (A/A) & Sanction

Applied during initial project planning and the Expenditure Finance Committee (EFC/SFC) phase. Ascertains preliminary budgetary reasonability before floating tenders, ensuring realistic budget allocations and preventing cost overruns.

Stage 2

Pre-Commercial Bid Opening Benchmark

Applied by the Tender Committee immediately before unsealing commercial bid envelopes of technically qualified vendors. Establishes a sealed, independent price benchmark to detect vendor collusion or inflated bids.
