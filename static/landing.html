Statutory Application Stages

Two Mandatory Procurement Milestones

🔹 Stage 1: Administrative Approval (A/A) & Sanction

Applied during the initial project planning and Expenditure Finance Committee (EFC/SFC) phase. Ascertains preliminary budgetary reasonability before floating tenders, ensuring realistic budget allocations and preventing cost overruns.

🔸 Stage 2: Pre-Commercial Bid Opening Benchmark

Applied by the Tender Committee immediately before unsealing the commercial bid envelopes of technically qualified vendors. Establishes a sealed, independent price benchmark to detect vendor collusion or inflated bids.
